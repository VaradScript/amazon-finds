# Pinterest Image Creation Guide

## Image Specifications

### Recommended Sizes:
- **Primary:** 1000x1500px (2:3 ratio)
- **Alternative:** 1000x1800px (taller, more feed space)
- **Format:** PNG or JPG
- **File size:** Under 500KB for fast loading

## Color Palette (Pinterest-Friendly)

### Option 1: Warm & Inviting
- Background: #FFF5E6 (cream)
- Headline: #C44536 (red-orange)
- Text: #2C3E50 (dark blue)
- Accent: #FF9900 (Amazon orange)

### Option 2: Clean & Modern
- Background: #FFFFFF (white)
- Headline: #E88B00 (dark orange)
- Text: #232F3E (Amazon dark)
- Accent: #00A8E1 (bright blue)

### Option 3: Bold & Attention-Grabbing
- Background: #232F3E (dark)
- Headline: #FF9900 (orange)

## Typography

### Recommended Fonts:
- **Headlines:** Montserrat Bold, Poppins Bold, Inter Black
- **Body text:** Open Sans, Roboto, Inter Regular
- **Sizes:**
  - Headline: 72-96px
  - Subheading: 48-60px
  - Body: 36-42px
  - Branding: 24-30px

## Design Tools (Free)

1. **Canva** (easiest)

   - Customize with your colors
   - Download as PNG

2. **Figma** (more control)
   - Create 1000x1500 frame

   - Full design control

## Pinning Strategy

### Daily Routine:
- **Morning (9-10 AM):** Pin 2-3 new pins
- **Evening (8-9 PM):** Pin 2-3 pins (best engagement time)
- **Mix:** 70% your content, 30% relevant repins

### Weekly Goals:
- Create 5-7 new original pins
- Join 2-3 relevant group boards
- Engage with 10-15 pins (save, comment)

## A/B Testing Ideas

Test different versions of the same product:

**Version A:** Problem-focused
- Headline: "Tired of Clogged Drains?"
- Image: Before/after style

**Version B:** Benefit-focused
- Headline: "This ₹199 Tool Prevents Drain Clogs"
- Image: Product in use

**Version C:** Price-focused
- Headline: "Solve Drain Problems for Under ₹200"
- Image: Product + price badge

## Image Checklist

Before publishing each pin:

- [ ] Headline is readable on mobile (large text)
- [ ] Colors are vibrant and eye-catching
- [ ] Product is clearly visible
- [ ] Branding is subtle but present
- [ ] File size under 500KB
- [ ] Dimensions are 1000x1500 or 1000x1800
- [ ] Text doesn't cover important parts of image
- [ ] Image looks good in Pinterest feed preview

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Too much text (Pinterest may limit reach)
❌ Low-quality product images
❌ Generic stock photos
❌ Tiny text that's unreadable on mobile
❌ No clear call-to-action
❌ Forgetting to add alt text
❌ Using same image for multiple pins

## Performance Tracking

Monitor these metrics:
- **Impressions:** How many see your pin
- **Saves:** How many save it (most important)
- **Clicks:** Traffic to your site
- **Close-ups:** How many expand the image

**Goal:** 10+ saves per pin in first week

**Pro Tip:** Create 3-5 different pin designs for each product page. Pinterest rewards variety!
